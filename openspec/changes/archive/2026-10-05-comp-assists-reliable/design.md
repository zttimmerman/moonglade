# Design: comp.assists reliability
- Lead (verifier round 3, main-comp1): the picked creature was in combat with guid 41 (another unit) and killed it; the master was never attacked.
- Options for the builder to evaluate:
  1. choose a creature that isn't in combat and has no other player/bot within N yd, re-checked just before engaging;
  2. spawn a temporary creature via GM at the staging point (despawn after), so nothing else has a claim on it;
  3. make the master initiate the fight in a way that doesn't count as the master attacking (if the spec allows). The spec says the master must not damage the creature, so be careful.
- Keep all existing counterexamples (passive companion, stay/not near, master attacks) failing.

## Root cause (B20, from evidence)
- **Guid 41 is the companion.** In `harness/out/verify3/main-comp1-results.json` the same evidence has `add.bot.guid = 41` (Twunveyglo, the warrior companion). The same holds in both earlier failures (`env/b19/v3full-fail-results.json`, `env/b19/comp2/results.json`) and in every recorded run: the addclass pool hands out the same bot. So "another unit" was our own companion. The creature wasn't fighting anything before. It attacked the companion instead of the master, and the companion killed it (the 36-damage hit with 26 overkill is the companion's; then the creature's `attack_stop` has `now_dead=1`).
- **Timing.** In all three failures the master landed and nothing attacked it. The creature's first combat event was `attack_start` on the companion 3.6 s, 3.6 s and 6.8 s after the master's teleport, which is when the following companion reached it. In passing runs the creature attacks the master 0.0–0.9 s after landing, with a few slow runs at 4.1–5.5 s.
- **Why the master wasn't attacked.** The check teleported the master to the position `.npc info` gave **when the creature was picked**, before the staging teleport, the summon and the near-wait. The picked creatures are wandering Mangy Wolves: `creature.MovementType = 1`, `wander_distance = 10` for spawns 81042, 81084 and 80810. Of the 40 nearest rule candidates, only one is stationary. By engage time the wolf had moved. Diagnostic replays without a companion (`env/b20/diag.py`, logs below) measured the drift from pick to engage at 0.3–11 yd. From such a stale point the wolf sometimes doesn't attack. In 14 replays aggro took 0.0–0.9 s in 12, but 2.8 s and 6.6 s in 2. In the 2.8 s case the wolf walked 6.6 → 4.6 → 2.7 yd toward the master, well inside its ~18 yd aggro radius (`Creature::GetAggroRange`: DetectionRange 20 minus the level difference), before it attacked. So `Creature::CanStartAttack` rejected the master from those points. Candidates are line of sight (Elwynn's trees are vmap objects; `CanStartAttack` ends with `IsWithinLOSInMap`) and the notifier race in `AIRelocationNotifier::Visit`, which skips a creature that has a pending relocation notify, as a moving one does. `.debug los` isn't available to the companion account, so which of the two it was isn't proven. It doesn't matter for the fix: both go away when the master stands on a stationary creature. With the companion present, a slow aggro becomes a failure as soon as the companion arrives first (≥ 3.6 s).
- **Control.** Teleporting onto the creature's *live* position while it stands still: aggro at 0.30–0.31 s in 5 of 5 replays (`env/b20/diag-gate.txt`, mode `still`). That is the first AI-notify tick.

## Fix (harness only)
- `companion.READY_RULE` / `Session.wait_creature_ready`: at the staging point, after the companion is near, poll `.npc info <spawn>` until the creature is **standing still**: two consecutive positions, ≥ `READY_POLL_S` (0.3 s) apart, within 0.05 yd. It must also be **alive at full health**, with **Unit Flags lacking `UNIT_FLAG_IN_COMBAT`** (0x80000, `UnitDefines.h`), and **no probe combat event** (SMSG_ATTACKSTART or combat log) with it as attacker or victim since the staging point. A wander pause comes within seconds: in the 15-run acceptance the gate waited 1.3–15.2 s (median 2.1 s), and then the creature attacked the master 0.30 s after landing in 14 runs and 0.82 s in 1, with no re-teleport needed. The wait is bounded at 30 s. If another unit is fighting the creature, the wait lasts until the fight ends, and fails as "creature died" if it dies.
- The master is then teleported onto that **live** position. If the creature hasn't attacked within `ASSIST_RETRY_S` (2 s, before the companion can arrive), the master is put back onto its current position, up to the existing 15 s bound. The master still never attacks: nothing is sent between landing and being attacked except `.go`, the teleport ack and one position heartbeat per landing.
- **Set aside busy candidates instead of failing.** The first 15-run attempt of the gate alone (`env/b20/rep-v1-*`: 5 of 9 passed) exposed three more ways the GIVEN failed:
  - A **Stormwind Guard** (entry 1423, a patrolling guard) attacked and killed the wolf while the master waited at the staging point (rep-v1-5). This is the "other unit" case the spec names. It also happens without us.
  - After back-to-back runs had killed the nearby wolves (3 min respawn), the next candidate was a waypoint-walking murloc (`creature.MovementType = 2`, spawn 81033), which never stood still in 30 s (rep-v1-4).
  - "no staging point matches the rule" for a crowded candidate (rep-v1-3, rep-v1-6; also seen before in `harness/out/v15/assists-1`).

  So the check now loops over up to `ASSIST_PICK_TRIES` (4) candidates. A candidate with no staging point, or one that dies, gets hurt or enters combat, or doesn't stand still within `READY_S` while the master waits at its staging point, is recorded in `creatures_set_aside` (with the combat events that made it busy). The next nearest live candidate is then tried. The creature rule drops waypoint walkers (`MovementType <> 2`, MotionMaster.h `WAYPOINT_MOTION_TYPE`). `pick_live_creature` also skips candidates that are in combat right now, and considers the nearest 20 rule spawns instead of 10.
- New evidence and requirements, all from this run:
  - `creature_ready`: the gate's samples.
  - `creature_before_fight`: health, max health, unit flags, `in_combat` and the combat events since staging, required `in_combat == false`, full health and no events. This is "the chosen creature wasn't in combat with any other unit before the fight".
  - `creature_first_attack`: the creature's first attack event since staging, which must target the master.
  - The unit that attacks the master must be the picked creature. Before, any creature matching the rule was accepted; this is a tightening.
- Option 2 (a temporary GM spawn) was rejected. `.npc add` writes a permanent spawn to the world DB. A temp summon runs `AIRelocationNotifier(..., includePlayers=true)` (Object.cpp SummonCreature) and engages whichever nearby player it visits first, so it would race the master against the companion. Option 3 conflicts with "the master doesn't act".

## Edits to the existing check (not loosenings)
- The engage position changed from the position at pick time to a live, stationary position. The WHEN is still "master teleported onto the creature; it attacks the master".
- The re-teleport retry only repeats the WHEN's own action before the master is attacked. Everything from the attack on is checked exactly as before: the same allowed opcodes after combat, the 20 s engage bound, death within 60 s, and no master damage.
- New requirements: the creature is out of combat before the fight; its first target is the master; the attacker is the picked creature. These only add failure modes.

## Counterexamples (B20, `env/b20/cx_assists.py`)
- `passive` (companion `co/nc +passive`): FAIL "companion not in combat with the creature within 20 s of the master".
- `not_near` (companion `stay`, no `.summon`): FAIL "precondition: companion not within 10 yd of the master at the staging point".
- `master_attacks` (CMSG_ATTACKSWING on selection): FAIL "the master damaged the creature".
- `busy_creature` (new evidence): the readiness gate is sabotaged to accept any standing creature, and the companion is told `attack` on the creature before the WHEN. Result: FAIL "precondition: the creature was in combat with another unit before the fight", caught by the companion's SMSG_ATTACKSTART on it.
- `busy_creature_flag`: the same, but with the probe's combat events hidden, so only the server's state can tell. Result: the same FAIL, caught by Unit Flags 557056 (IN_COMBAT set) and health 84/120.
